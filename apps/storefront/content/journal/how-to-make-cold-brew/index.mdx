Cold brew is easier than it sounds and a lot harder to mess up. No heat, no rush—just time doing the heavy lifting while you get on with your day.

The result is smooth, naturally sweet, and low in acidity. Brew it once and you have clean, bold coffee ready for the fridge all week.

## Start with the ratio.

<IngredientList>

- 1 cup coarsely ground coffee
- 4 cups cold filtered water

</IngredientList>

The reliable starting point is one part coffee to four parts water. It makes a concentrate with enough backbone for ice, water, or milk without turning murky or bitter.

## How to make it.

<RecipeSteps>
  <RecipeStep number={1} title="Combine.">
    Add the coffee and water to a large jar or pitcher. Give it a good stir
    until all the grounds are wet.
  </RecipeStep>
  <RecipeStep number={2} title="Leave it alone.">
    Cover and steep in the fridge for 8 to 12 hours. A longer steep makes a
    bolder cup, but more is not always better.
  </RecipeStep>
  <RecipeStep number={3} title="Strain and pour.">
    Strain through a fine mesh sieve or coffee filter. Store the finished
    concentrate in the fridge for up to seven days.
  </RecipeStep>
</RecipeSteps>

<PullQuote>
  Cold brew should taste like coffee. Colder. That’s the whole pitch.
</PullQuote>

## A note on serving.

Cold brew is concentrated. Start with equal parts coffee and water or milk, then adjust to your taste. Pour it over plenty of ice and do not overthink it.

<MascotTip>Start over ice, then add water or milk to taste.</MascotTip>

## Why it works.

Cool water extracts coffee more slowly than hot water. That slower extraction leaves behind some of the sharpest compounds while still pulling out sweetness and body. Coarse grounds keep the brew easy to strain and help prevent a silty finish.

### Can I use pre-ground coffee?

Yes. Look for a coarse grind if you can. A standard medium grind still works, but you may need a paper filter after the first strain.

### Does the roast matter?

Any roast works. Medium roast is the plainest place to start: enough sweetness for drinking black, enough weight to stand up to milk.
